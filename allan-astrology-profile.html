— V —

How I Show Up at Work

Communication

Considered, then concrete.

Mercury in Taurus + Pluto in the 3rd: I'd rather take a beat and respond well than respond fast. When I do speak, I tend to go a layer deeper than expected. Don't read the silence as disengagement — it's usually drafting.

Collaboration

Quiet glue, patient bridge.

Virgo Rising and Venus in Pisces make me a natural translator between people who don't quite share a vocabulary — particularly between operations and engineering. I show care through usefulness, not declarations.

Leadership & Ownership

Builder more than commander.

Aries Sun + Capricorn Moon + Saturn-in-Capricorn: I lead by setting up the structure, then trusting people inside it. I'd rather own the system than micromanage the steps. I'll start things — but I'm not loud about starting them.

Decision-Making

Earth-weighted, time-tested.

Four Earth placements mean I want to see the evidence and feel the ground. Mutable energy keeps me from over-committing too soon. I'll change direction when new information arrives — but I won't change direction because someone is louder.

Creativity & Problem-Solving

Inventive within constraints.

Mars in Aquarius in the craft-house means I get genuinely excited about non-obvious solutions — automations, AI tools, cleaner workflows. The fun for me is "how could this work differently and still hold up?"

How I Like to Be Managed

Trust the build, share the why.

Tell me the goal and the constraint, then let me design the path. I don't need a lot of cheerleading — but I do need to know what we're actually trying to do, why it matters, and where the edges are.

— THE SHORT VERSION —

How the pieces fit together

The most useful frame for my chart is a tension that mostly resolves into a strength: an Aries Sun wants to start things and move quickly, but it's nested inside a chart that is overwhelmingly earthy and Capricornian. So the spark exists, but it almost never stays as raw spark. It gets routed through structure, craft, and patience before it shows up in public.

Add a Virgo Rising and the picture becomes: someone who is internally a starter, but externally reads as a careful builder. The Aries energy goes into building things that didn't exist before — workflows, tools, frameworks — rather than into being the loudest voice in the room.

The Capricorn stellium — Moon, Uranus, Neptune all clustered with Saturn nearby — is the heart of how I work. Imagination, security, and reform all live in the same "structural" room, which is why building tools and reinventing systems feels like one coherent thing to me, not four different hobbies. Mars in Aquarius in the 6th gives that whole apparatus a forward-looking edge: I'm restless about how work itself gets done, and I'd rather redesign the process than tolerate a broken one.

What energizes me

  • Building something from a blank page — tools, systems, frameworks, decks
  • Translating between operations and engineering vocabulary
  • Quiet, focused depth-work where craft can compound
  • Problems that look impossible until you reframe the structure
  • Trust + autonomy + a clear constraint to push against

What can challenge me

  • Performative urgency, where speed is valued over correctness
  • Being pushed to commit publicly before I've thought it through
  • Environments that mistake quietness for low engagement
  • Letting "build the better system" eclipse "ship the okay one"
  • Carrying weight invisibly — Capricorn Moon doesn't always ask for help

A snapshot, not a verdict. Astrology is most useful here as a shared vocabulary — a way to surface working preferences without flattening them into a four-letter code. I'd rather you read this and recognize me, than read it and predict me.
